| Re-tapping the current slot **when already at root** | *silent* | Nothing changed |

Four things worth drawing out:

**The `reject` calls are the interesting ones.** Giving Reset and Forget a different weight from an ordinary tap is what makes the haptics informative rather than merely present — you feel that something was thrown away. Both are judgement calls: `confirm` is defensible for Forget, since the dialog's whole point is that your file is untouched. I'd start with `reject` and it's a one-word change either way.

**A `reload()` trap this app is unusually exposed to.** B14 says put the call at the interaction site, not the ViewModel, because "the same ViewModel callback may be reached from a tap, a restore, or another app's write". `reload()` is called by the toolbar Refresh button *and* by `onResume()`. Putting the haptic in the ViewModel would make the app buzz every time you switch back to it. The tap belongs in the `IconButton`.

**The shared row components make this cheap.** Nearly every tap in the app already funnels through `ListRow`, `NavigationRow`, `ChoiceRow` and `SwitchRow`. Adding a `sensation: Sensation = Tap` parameter to `ListRow` gives roughly six call sites instead of thirty, with the Reset and Forget rows passing `Reject` explicitly. `checklists` needed 32 call sites; this app's structure should need far fewer.

**Both API-30 constants are in play.** `CONFIRM` and `REJECT` both need the version guard and the `LONG_PRESS` fallback at `minSdk 26`. And per the appendix, taps take `VIRTUAL_KEY`, never `CONTEXT_CLICK`.
